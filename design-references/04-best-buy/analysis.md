# Best Buy: UX analysis

**Evidence basis:** DOM facts from `facts.json`. Screenshots are clean but were not reviewed closely for this draft.

## 1. Store context in the header
- "Your store Find a store" appears in the desktop header.
- **Why:** Best Buy stock and pickup depend on the local store. The label gives a direct route to set it.

## 2. Account and Shop as primary header items
- The header lists "Account Sign in", "Shop", "Deals", "Support & Services", and "Discover".
- **Why:** the labels separate buying (Shop, Deals) from help (Support & Services), which matches the two main intents on an electronics store.

## 3. Mobile: Menu, Sign in, and an account prompt
- The phone header shows Menu, Sign in, and search. A "Create an account" prompt sits in the content.
- **Why:** sign-in and account creation are offered at two points, which catches both returning and new shoppers.

## 4. Feedback and survey prompts
- An "Assistive Survey" control appears in the header.
- **Why:** an in-page survey collects feedback during the session, not after it. The trade-off is that it adds a control to the header.

## Lessons for a re-skin
- Show the local store or delivery context in the header.
- Separate "Shop" from "Support" in the top navigation.

## What not to copy
- Survey prompts in the header compete with the main actions. Keep feedback out of the primary bar.
