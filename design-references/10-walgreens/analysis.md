# Walgreens: UX analysis

**Evidence basis:** DOM facts from `facts.json`. Screenshots are clean but were not reviewed closely for this draft.

## 1. Pharmacy first in the navigation
- "Pharmacy" and "Health Services" sit next to "Shop", "Savings", and "Photo" in the top navigation.
- **Why:** prescriptions and health tasks are high-urgency, so they are placed before general retail. This order matches the shopper's reason for visiting.

## 2. Nearest store always visible
- "Nearest store" with the store's address is in the header.
- **Why:** pharmacy pickup and in-store services need a store. Showing the address keeps the context in view.

## 3. Search that accepts item numbers
- The search placeholder reads "Search by keyword or item number".
- **Why:** shoppers reorder by item number, so accepting it avoids a separate reorder flow.

## 4. Scheduling as a prominent action on mobile
- "Schedule now" appears on the phone view.
- **Why:** appointment-based services (vaccines, tests) need a direct action.

## Lessons for a re-skin
- Put the most urgent service in the top navigation, if your business has one.
- Let search accept product codes.

## What not to copy
- The navigation has many items. Group the less-used services under one menu.
