# Home Depot: UX analysis

**Evidence basis:** DOM facts from `facts.json`. The phone home view has a fixed bottom bar, confirmed in the facts. Screenshots are clean but were not reviewed closely for this draft.

## 1. An assistant in the header
- "Ask Magic Apron" appears in the header.
- **Why:** home projects are often unclear to the shopper. A guided assistant reduces the search-by-guessing problem. Labelling it "Ask" sets the expectation of a conversation.

## 2. Financing as a visible offer
- "Home Depot Credit Cards" appears in the top bar.
- **Why:** big-ticket items (appliances, tools, flooring) are often bought on credit. Showing the offer early lets shoppers plan the purchase.

## 3. Local hours and store
- "The Dalles 9PM" appears next to the zip code.
- **Why:** store hours decide whether a shopper can pick up today. Showing the closing time answers that question directly.

## 4. Persistent bottom bar on phones
- On mobile, a fixed bottom bar holds "Shop All", "Services", "DIY", and "Log In".
- **Why:** the thumb reaches the bottom of the screen most easily. Keeping the main categories there reduces scrolling back to the top.

## Lessons for a re-skin
- A fixed bottom bar with four to five main destinations works well on phones.
- Show store hours and pickup availability in the header.

## What not to copy
- The top bar carries an assistant, a credit offer, a store, and a zip code. Each adds a decision. Keep only the ones that change the purchase.
